## Publisher

I am [Frank Lämmer](https://www.franklaemmer.de), co-founder of a small bootstrapped hosting provider called [fortrabbit](/providers/fortrabbit). The main data dates back to so competitor research I did for our new hosting platform in 2023. It was ported and extended to a 'hosting guide' on our fortrabbit website. But I found, it deserved it's own space. If our record reads more favorably than the evidence supports.

## Your benefits

A lot of developers don't know much about web hosting. It's a market of lemons. Learn how to judge better. Discover a hosting service that really fit's your requirements.

- Open. Contributions welcome.
- Fair. Pushing smaller, indie providers

## What this is not

Sometimes, something is best described by what it is not:

- Hosting rank, score or rate.
- Hosting price tracker. Price bands only.

## Credits

AI agents gathered facts. Much of the code is written by AI. Frank has reviewed all pages and designed the data structure. Built with [Astro](https://astro.build), content schema. Search is [Pagefind](https://pagefind.app), which indexes the built HTML rather than a database.
