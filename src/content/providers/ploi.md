## About Ploi

Ploi is a [server management software](/category/server-management/), originally built with Laravel. It's from the Netherlands and was self-funded around 2018. One-click installs for WordPress, October CMS and others are offered. Ploi now also offers [Ploi Cloud](/provider/ploi-cloud/).

Like other tools in this category, Ploi is a control panel, not a host. You bring your own VPS — DigitalOcean, Hetzner, Vultr, AWS and the like — and Ploi provisions and configures it for you. That distinction is the whole story when you compare it to a managed platform.

## The responsibility gap

Ploi sets the box up, but you still own it. Once the server is provisioned, the kernel and OS updates, security patches, backups, monitoring, log rotation, failed disks and the 3am incident are all yours. Ploi automates the setup and hands you the keys — what happens to the box after that is your operations work, not theirs.

## You pay more than one bill

With Ploi you pay at least twice: the Ploi subscription for the panel, plus the VPS provider for the actual compute — and often a third bill for off-server backups, object storage or a managed database once the project grows. Each lives in a separate account with separate support and separate invoices.
