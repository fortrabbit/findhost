## About Ploi

Like other tools in this category, Ploi is a control panel, not a host. You bring your own VPS — DigitalOcean, Hetzner, Vultr, AWS and the like — and Ploi provisions and configures it for you. That distinction is the whole story when you compare it to a managed platform.
