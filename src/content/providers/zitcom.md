Zitcom Group was a Danish holding company for hosting brands sold to small and medium businesses — Wannafind, ScanNet and UnoEuro among them — rather than a host selling under its own name. Combell Group acquired it in 2017, as the [team.blue newsroom](https://press.team.blue/160534-zitcom-group-and-combell-join-forces/) recorded at the time, and Combell merged with TransIP two years later to form [team.blue](/holdings/team-blue/).

Nothing is sold under the Zitcom name today, and there is no longer a company site to read: `zitcom.dk` and `www.zitcom.dk` both redirect to team.blue's Danish careers page. The brands the group assembled are run by team.blue's Danish operation, each still trading under its own name.
