## About Hostinger

Hostinger started in 2004 in Kaunas, Lithuania, as Hosting Media and took the Hostinger name in 2011. Today it is one of the largest budget hosts worldwide, serving tens of millions of customers. The line-up covers shared web hosting, cloud hosting, VPS, managed WordPress hosting, email hosting, domains and an AI website builder — all managed through hPanel, Hostinger's own control panel.

Shared hosting is sold on a 48-month prepayment at an introductory rate, with the renewal price printed alongside it: Premium at $2.99 a month renewing at $10.99, Unlimited at $3.79 renewing at $16.99, Cloud Startup at $7.99 renewing at $25.99.

## Reservations

The advertised price requires paying four years up front, and the entry plan then renews at roughly 3.7 times that rate. The figure a buyer compares is not the figure they end up paying, and the gap is larger here than at any other host in this dataset that publishes both numbers.

Hostinger is built for consumers and small businesses: penny entry prices, a website builder, one-click installers. The workflow centers on hPanel and FTP-style file management, not on version control. Professional PHP development — Git deploys, Composer, separate staging environments — is not what the product is optimized for.
