BitFolk sells one thing: an unmanaged Linux virtual server with root access, IPv6 and no control panel wrapped around it. There is no shared hosting and no managed tier, which is unusual enough to be the description rather than a limitation.

## Worth knowing

It is a small operation, and the customers who write about it name the founder. That is the trade being offered — support from somebody who knows the machine, in place of a rota that answers at three in the morning.

The kind of host this register exists to make findable: no marketing budget, no affiliate programme, and invisible in a search for the category it belongs to.
