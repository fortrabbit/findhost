## About A2 Hosting

A2 Hosting was a classical shared hosting provider from Ann Arbor, Michigan, founded in 2001 and known for its "Turbo" speed tiers. World Host Group acquired the company in January 2025, and in April 2025 the brand was retired in favor of Hosting.com. The product range — shared hosting, VPS, dedicated servers, managed WordPress, international server locations — continues under the new name. We don't have first hand experience.

## Reservations

The A2 Hosting brand no longer exists: accounts, bookmarks, and support contacts now lead to Hosting.com, one of more than 25 hosting brands in the World Host Group portfolio. As usual in the segment, introductory prices renew at higher rates, and shared hosting is made for consumers, not for professional web developers — deployment pipelines and comparable developer tooling are missing.
