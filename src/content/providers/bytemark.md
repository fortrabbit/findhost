Bytemark was a UK provider of virtual private servers, running them on infrastructure it called BigV, and one of the longer-standing independent hosts in the country before it was bought.

The buyer is Hosting UK, a division of Easyspace Ltd, which has imported Bytemark accounts into its own systems and moved domains and billing across. New services are sold under the Hosting UK name rather than Bytemark's, and support runs through the buyer's portal. The bytemark.co.uk domain now redirects to Hosting UK's page about the move, and nothing is sold under the Bytemark name any more.
