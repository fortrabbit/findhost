iwantmyname is a small registrar, trading since 2008, selling names across several hundred extensions with DNS management included. Its distinguishing feature is the setup it does after the purchase: a list of one-click DNS templates for the services a domain typically points at, so connecting a name to a mail host or a site builder is a choice from a list rather than a set of records typed by hand.

## Worth knowing

No price is recorded here, and not for want of looking: the domain prices load into the page after a search rather than being printed on it, so nothing could be read directly. Prices are quoted in five currencies.

Nothing of a customer's runs here. It sells the name and answers for it, and the site lives on a [host](/categories/paas/) somewhere else.
