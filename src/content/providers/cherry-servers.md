## About Cherry Servers

Cherry Servers has run since 2001 from Lithuania and sells dedicated hardware from about €0.084 an hour and virtual servers from €0.015, with elastic block and backup storage, floating IPs, load balancers and public IP subnets alongside. Billing can be hourly, fixed-term or spot, which is a wider set of options than most providers of its size offer.

Data centres are in the Netherlands, Lithuania, Germany, Sweden, Chicago, Singapore and Tokyo. Technical support is free and staffed around the clock, the availability commitment is 99.97%, and there is a fifteen-day money-back guarantee. More than twenty payment methods are accepted including several cryptocurrencies — a detail that says something about which markets it serves, alongside the named ones of Web3, gaming, AI, streaming and trading.

## Reservations

This is infrastructure with no managed application layer: the operating system and everything above it belong to the customer. There is no free tier. The Lithuanian location — one of the more interesting facts about the company — is visible in the data-centre list rather than stated as a headquarters, and no ownership information is published on the pricing page.
