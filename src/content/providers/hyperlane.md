Hyperlane is a managed hosting platform for Craft CMS and WordPress sites. It is a commercial offering of DeltaBlue NV, a company registered in Hasselt, Belgium, as the [terms and conditions](https://hyperlane.co/terms/conditions) state.

Each project gets development, staging and production environments, a Git repository and automatic certificates. Code reaches a project by Git or by SFTP. Plans are priced per site and month in fixed tiers, and the [pricing page](https://hyperlane.co/pricing) offers development and staging environments free for a limited time before a paid plan is needed.

## Worth knowing

Unused free projects are removed: the pricing page asks for a check-in at least once a month, and a project with no paid plan is deleted when the free period ends.
