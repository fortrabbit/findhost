## About Vultr

Vultr is a cloud infrastructure provider founded in 2014. They offer virtual private servers, bare metal servers, and cloud compute instances across a large number of global data center locations. Vultr is known for competitive pricing on raw VPS compute and NVMe SSD storage, and has expanded its product line to include managed Kubernetes, object storage, and GPU instances for AI workloads. Their target audience is developers and businesses that want full control over their infrastructure.

## Reservations

Vultr gives you a Linux server and root access — and that is where the managed experience ends. Everything else is on you: installing a web server, configuring PHP, setting up SSL certificates, hardening security, applying OS patches, and monitoring uptime. That is a significant ongoing time investment, separate from actually building your application. Developers who underestimate this often end up with insecure, out-of-date servers or spend hours on infrastructure tasks instead of shipping features.
