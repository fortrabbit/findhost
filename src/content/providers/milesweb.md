## About MilesWeb

MilesWeb has hosted from Nashik in Maharashtra since 2012 and states more than a million websites on its platform. Four shared plans run from ₹69 a month for one site to ₹499 for a hundred, stepping up on NVMe storage, mailboxes and Node.js application slots.

Two things stand out for a host at this price. The first is the specification: PHP is selectable from 5.6 to 8.5, Node.js applications are supported on every tier, and SSH and git access come as standard rather than as an upsell. The second is the commercial promise — renewal at the original rate, which is the opposite of the industry's usual pattern and worth more than most of the features around it.

Administration runs through mPanel, the company's own dashboard, and servers are offered in India alongside the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and Singapore.

## Reservations

The proprietary panel is a switching cost: a site set up in mPanel does not move to a cPanel host by restoring a backup. Databases are capped on the lower tiers. The renewal guarantee is stated on the pricing page rather than in visible terms, so it is a marketing commitment rather than a documented one.
