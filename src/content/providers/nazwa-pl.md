Nazwa.pl is a Polish hosting provider, part of NetArt Group which has operated globally since 1997. The company offers cloud hosting under the CloudHosting brand, alongside domains, email services, virtual private servers and managed databases. Cloud hosting runs PHP 8.2 through 8.5 with Node.js available in LTS versions. All plans include CDN integration, DDoS and WAF protection with an uptime SLA of 99.9%.

Entry-level cloud hosting starts at 75 PLN per month and includes domain registration and email services. Domain registration alone is available across over 300 extensions, with the .pl extension offered free for the first year. Storage, CPU and RAM scale across three main tiers.

## Worth knowing

Pricing is displayed in Polish zloty only, with no multi-currency billing option published.
