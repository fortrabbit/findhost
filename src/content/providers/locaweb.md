Locaweb is the anchor brand of LWSA, a Brazilian technology group listed on the B3 exchange, hosting a stated 500,000-plus sites. Shared plans run PHP with a documented version switcher, SSH is enabled from the panel in three-hour windows on Linux plans, and prices are published in reais.

Locaweb publishes no API, CLI or infrastructure-as-code path, which is unusual at this size. Its sibling brand [KingHost](/kinghost/) runs on the same group infrastructure.
