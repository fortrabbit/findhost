## About Hosting.com

Hosting.com is the company formerly called A2 Hosting. A2 Hosting was founded in 2003 in Ann Arbor, Michigan, and was known for its "Turbo" speed tiers; World Host Group combined it with its other brands and retired the A2 Hosting name in 2025 in favour of a single Hosting.com brand. Anyone searching for A2 Hosting is looking at this company: `a2hosting.com` now redirects to `hosting.com`, and existing accounts, bookmarks and support contacts follow.

The product range carried over — shared hosting, VPS, dedicated servers, reseller hosting, managed WordPress, domains and SSL — as did the international footprint. The company states it serves customers in 181 countries from offices including Ann Arbor, Bali, Sofia, Manila and Bangalore, having grown through acquisitions such as Kenya Web Experts, EYHost and Rocket.net. Shared hosting is advertised from $2.99 a month.

## Reservations

Shared hosting in this segment is sold on an introductory price that renews higher, and the advertised figure is normally tied to a multi-year prepayment. The renewal rate is not shown next to the sign-up price on the plan pages, so the second-term cost is not visible at the point of decision.

The product is also built for site owners rather than developers: deployment pipelines, staging environments and comparable tooling are not part of the shared hosting offer.
