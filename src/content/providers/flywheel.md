## About Flywheel

Flywheel is a managed WordPress host built for web designers, freelancers, and agencies. Founded in 2012 in Omaha, Nebraska, it was acquired by [WP Engine](/provider/wpengine/) in 2019 and now runs as a WP Engine brand — still with its own dashboard, plans, and designer-focused workflow. Sites run on Google Cloud with a Fastly-powered CDN, across data centers in North America, the UK, the EU, and Australia.

The agency workflow is the draw: client billing transfer, collaborator roles, reusable site blueprints, free demo sites, and the free Local app for building WordPress offline. Plans — Starter, Freelance, Agency — bundle staging, nightly backups, and free SSL. Like most managed WordPress hosts, Flywheel keeps a list of disallowed plugins (mostly caching, backup, and security plugins that clash with its stack) and offers SSH through a restricted gateway rather than full shell access.
