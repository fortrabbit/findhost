hosting.de GmbH is a German hosting company based in Aachen, per its [imprint](https://www.hosting.de/ueber-uns/impressum/). It sells shared web hosting for PHP applications, cloud and managed servers, domain registration with DNS, business email and managed Nextcloud.

Web hosting comes in fixed tiers with daily backups included, and the [web hosting page](https://www.hosting.de/webhosting/) names data centers in Aachen and Frankfurt. A public API covers domains and DNS.

## Worth knowing

The advertised web hosting prices assume yearly billing.
