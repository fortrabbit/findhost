A group of hosting brands acquired and run under their own names, which renamed itself Hosting.com in 2025 — the record under the name it trades by is [Hosting.com](/hosting-com/). Nobody bought hosting from World Host Group; they bought it from a brand it owned.

[Oakley Capital](/holdings/oakley-capital/) backed the group and merged its Webcentral business into it in 2024, and its [portfolio entry](https://www.oakleycapital.com/our-companies/hostingcom) is where the rename is recorded.
