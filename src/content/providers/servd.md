## About Servd

Servd.host is a hosting service specialized in Craft CMS. It is uniquely made for Craft and has therefore a high grade of abstraction for doing just that. Run by a small, independent, UK-based team and publisher of its own Craft plugin, Servd has a strong reputation in the Craft community as the original Craft-focused platform.

Under the hood, Servd runs containers on Kubernetes with atomic git-push deploys, a Craft-tuned stack (Nginx, PHP 8, Redis, static caching), and data centers in the US, EU, UK, and Canada. The Craft-specific features are the draw: off-server image transforms, a global asset CDN, automated backups with one-click restore, and staging on every plan. SSH is available through short-lived sessions, and the database is reached over an SSH tunnel. Each plan hosts a single Craft install.
