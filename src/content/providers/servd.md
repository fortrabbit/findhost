## About Servd

Servd is "Built for Craft CMS" — a managed platform whose infrastructure is arranged around Craft rather than a general-purpose host that happens to run it. It is run by a small independent team that also publishes its own Craft plugin.

Three plans are sold, each hosting one Craft installation: Starter with 2 CPUs, 5 PHP workers, 2 GB of asset storage and 200 GB a month of image bandwidth; Enhanced with 3.5 CPUs, 10 workers, 5 GB and 450 GB; Pro with 5 CPUs, 15 workers, 10 GB and 700 GB. Custom and enterprise arrangements are available on request. Every plan includes fully managed infrastructure, git integration, a staging environment, daily backups with 30-day retention, WAF and DDoS protection and automatic crash recovery. Regions offered are the US, the EU, the UK and Canada.

The stated audience is "forward-thinking developers", teams running medium-sized sites, and agencies and studios managing several projects at once.

## Reservations

A plan is one Craft installation. An agency running several sites buys several plans, so the per-site cost does not fall with volume the way it does on a platform that sells capacity by the resource.

The specialisation cuts both ways. Everything is arranged around Craft, which is why the platform is comfortable for a Craft build and why it is not somewhere to put the rest of a mixed portfolio. The pricing page also lists the EU as a region without naming which country it is in, which matters to anyone with a data-location requirement to satisfy.
