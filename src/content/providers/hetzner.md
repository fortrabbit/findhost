## About Hetzner

Hetzner is an independent German web hosting company founded in 1997, headquartered in Gunzenhausen. Expect to hear a Bavarian accent when calling their German speaking support.

They offer a broad range of hosting services: shared hosting, dedicated servers, colocation, VPS, storage and cloud. Unlike the major hyperscalers, Hetzner is privately held. Data centers are in Germany, Finland and the US.

## Reservations

Hetzner gives you raw infrastructure. A Linux server, that you are responsible for. That means configuring the web stack (NGINX/Apache, PHP-FPM, MySQL, SSL, firewalls), applying security patches, managing OS upgrades, and monitoring uptime yourself. The low price per hour reflects this trade-off: you pay less money but invest considerably more time. For teams without a dedicated sysadmin, that ongoing operational overhead can quietly outweigh the savings. It's not only the time, it's also responsibility. Configuring servers with AI is easier than ever, but will it not work and is it secure?
