## About DigitalOcean

DigitalOcean Holdings, Inc. is a technology company that provides cloud services and is traded on the American stock market (DOCN). The company initially started with offering VPS services, but as it grew, it expanded to offer IaaS cloud services to capture a share of the larger client market. Additionally, they now provide an app hosting platform that is partially integrated with PagodaBox. DigitalOcean also acquired [Cloudways](/provider/cloudways/).

## Reservations

DigitalOcean VPS may seem like an affordable option for hosting. Consider that it requires a significant investment of time and skills to setup and constantly manage. Developers who choose to handle their own hosting with VPS often underestimate the complexity of the task. Being Linux sysadmin is a time-consuming and challenging job.
