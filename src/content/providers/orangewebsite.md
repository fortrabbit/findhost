OrangeWebsite sells shared hosting, virtual servers and dedicated machines from Iceland, and the country is the product as much as the hardware is: the pitch is Icelandic speech law, an account opened with nothing but an email address, and payment in cryptocurrency if that is what the customer wants.

## Worth knowing

The green claim and the anonymity claim come from the same fact, which is easy to miss. Iceland's grid is geothermal and hydroelectric, so a server there is low-carbon whoever runs it — that is the country's doing rather than the provider's.

A jurisdiction is a promise about who can compel what, and it is worth reading the terms rather than the front page, since the guarantee is bounded by Icelandic law.
