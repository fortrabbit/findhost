## About Contabo

Contabo is a German hosting provider founded in 2003, headquartered in Munich. They are best known for low-cost VPS plans with generous RAM, storage, and bandwidth allocations at price points that undercut most of the market. The product range covers VPS, dedicated servers, object storage, and managed Kubernetes. The cheapest listed plan, Cloud VPS 4, is €5.50 a month for 4 vCPU cores, 8 GB of RAM, 100 GB of SSD and unlimited traffic at 200 Mbit/s — a rate the page describes as introductory for the first 24 months. The minimum initial contract is one month, and each following term matches the initial one. Snapshots are included; automatic backups are a paid add-on. Regions are listed as the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States, Singapore, Japan, India and Australia, without naming which EU country the European capacity sits in.

## Reservations

Contabo supplies a Linux box and root access; the web server, PHP, certificates, security patches, uptime monitoring and backups belong to the account holder. The headline price is low, and the administration time behind it is not part of the quote.

The headline price is also introductory. Contabo states that the €5.50 rate applies for the first 24 months but does not publish what it becomes afterwards, so the second-term cost cannot be read off the pricing page — which is why no renewal multiple is recorded here.
