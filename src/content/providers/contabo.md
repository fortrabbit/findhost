## About Contabo

Contabo is a German hosting provider founded in 2003, headquartered in Munich. They are best known for low-cost VPS plans with generous RAM, storage, and bandwidth allocations at price points that undercut most of the market. The product range covers VPS, dedicated servers, object storage, and managed Kubernetes. Data centers are spread across Germany, the US, the UK, Singapore, Japan, India, and Australia. The target audience is budget-conscious developers, hobbyists, and small businesses.

## Reservations

Contabo gives you a Linux box and root access — the rest is your problem. You configure the web server, install PHP, set up SSL, apply security patches, monitor uptime, and handle backups. The headline price is attractive, but the time you spend on server administration is rarely factored in. Reports about variable I/O performance on shared hypervisors and slow support response times are common. For a side project that is acceptable; for anything that needs to stay online and fast, the hidden cost adds up.
