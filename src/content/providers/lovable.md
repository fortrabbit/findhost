## About Lovable

Lovable is an AI-powered builder that turns natural language descriptions into working web applications. The AI generates React code, and Lovable handles deployment on its own infrastructure. It supports one-click publishing. It is aimed at non-developers and founders who want to move fast without having to hire developers. The platform runs on a credit model. Each AI interaction consumes credits.
