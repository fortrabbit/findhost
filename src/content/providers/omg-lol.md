omg.lol sells a personal address on its own domain: a subdomain that doubles as a profile page, an email-forwarding address and a handful of small web tools — a link shortener, a pastebin, a statuslog — under one account, as the [services overview](https://home.omg.lol/info/services) describes. The company, Neatnik, is based in Louisville, Kentucky, and the project grew out of a domain name registered in 2019, per the [About omg.lol](https://home.omg.lol/info/about) page.

Pricing is a single flat rate, published as a monthly figure billed annually. A member buys between one and ten years at a time, and the same rate applies to gift codes that someone else can redeem later.

## Worth knowing

Renewals are not automatic. Per the [billing article](https://home.omg.lol/info/billing), omg.lol does not retain payment details or auto-renew a subscription, and instead contacts a member ahead of expiry so they can renew by hand.

The address lives inside the omg.lol domain rather than one of the member's own choosing outside it, though its DNS is fully configurable, and it can be represented as an emoji instead of a word.
