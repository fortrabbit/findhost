The company behind the Deno runtime, which also operates Deno Deploy. The runtime is the product; the hosting is how it is sold. Its [blog post of October 9, 2026](https://deno.com/blog/cloudflare) announces that the team is joining Cloudflare, that Deno Deploy will shut down after six months, and that development of the runtime ends a year after the announcement.
