SporeStack sells virtual servers provisioned at DigitalOcean and Vultr, plus server options under its own name, and its [home page](https://sporestack.com/) says a server launched at an upstream provider has to conform to that provider's policies as well. The same page says there is no KYC and no email required: servers are bought against a prepaid token, and the whole service is driven through a public API. The cheapest plans on the [pricing page](https://sporestack.com/pricing/) are a slow-server tier, against a deposit.

## Worth knowing

The home page is explicit about the limits: it says the service is not "bulletproof" or DMCA-ignored hosting, that the company is still US-based, and that the DMCA applies.
