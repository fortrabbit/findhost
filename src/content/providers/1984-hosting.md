## About 1984 Hosting

1984 Hosting has operated from Iceland since 2006 under the banner "free speech and privacy since 2006", and reports more than 92,000 customers. The name is the argument: the company positions itself as an ethical and independent host, accepts Bitcoin and Monero, publishes a section on litigation it has been involved in, and is ISO 27001 certified.

Iceland is a deliberate choice on two counts. Its data-protection posture is one; the other is that the data centres run on geothermal and hydroelectric power, which is about as close to genuinely low-carbon electricity as hosting gets without buying certificates. Products are shared web hosting with one-click WordPress, VPS from a single core with root access and DDoS protection, and a free anycast DNS service with automatic DNSSEC and zone history.

## Reservations

Advertised prices carry very large standing discounts — the web hosting figure is shown against an 85% reduction — so the entry number is promotional rather than the standing rate. Runtimes beyond PHP, SSH availability on shared plans and database options are not stated on the pages that sell the product, so a developer has to ask rather than read.
