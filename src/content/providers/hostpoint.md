Hostpoint is a Swiss-based hosting provider offering shared hosting and managed servers. Plans start at CHF 15.90 per month for shared hosting with PHP 8.1, one-click installations for WordPress, Joomla, Drupal, and TYPO3, and included MariaDB databases. The Managed Flex Server tier adds Node.js, Java, Ruby on Rails, and Python support, with configurations from 4 to 16 vCPU and 4 to 96 GB RAM. All infrastructure operates within Switzerland, including datacenters in FINMA and ISO-27001 certified facilities.

Hostpoint emphasizes Swiss-based support and security, with phone support available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Backups range from 30 to 180 days depending on the plan tier. All plans include free SSL certificates.
