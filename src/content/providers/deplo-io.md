Deploio takes a git repository and runs it: push to a branch and the platform builds and serves the result, with a Dockerfile accepted instead where the detected runtime is not enough. It is operated by [nine](/nine-ch/), the Swiss provider whose other products are servers and Kubernetes rather than a platform.

## Worth knowing

The claim being sold is jurisdictional rather than technical: the infrastructure sits in Zurich and the company states that everything stays under Swiss law. For a buyer who needs that, it narrows the field to very few platforms.

No price is recorded. Both the product page and the pricing page publish a calculator rather than figures, so the starting cost cannot be read without configuring an application first.
