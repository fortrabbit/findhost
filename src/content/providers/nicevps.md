NiceVPS sells shared hosting, virtual servers, dedicated servers and domains, marketed as uncensored hosting. Its [home page](https://nicevps.net/) says it does not ask many questions at sign-up and that customers provide whatever data they want to share, and that the service runs from Dominica. The site does not name the company behind it, and no technical documentation was found.

## Worth knowing

The same page offers hosting for what it calls high-risk businesses and an anonymous SMTP relay.
