A US investment firm, recorded here because it is one of the two owners of [Newfold Digital](/holdings/newfold-digital/), the group that holds Bluehost, HostGator and Network Solutions. Clearlake completed its acquisition of Endurance International in 2021 and combined it with Web.com, which [Siris](/holdings/siris/) held, to form Newfold, as the group's [formation announcement](https://www.newfold.com/newsroom/clearlake-completes-acquisition-of-endurance-international-group) records. A December 2025 [financing release](https://www.newfold.com/newsroom/newfold-digital-secures--100-million-investment-to-accelerate-gr) names both firms as its backers still, and says the transaction did not alter that ownership. It has never sold hosting under its own name.
