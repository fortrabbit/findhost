TPC Hosting is a brand of TPC Management s.r.l. in Ilfov County, Romania, as its [terms](https://tpc-hosting.com/terms--conditions) state. It sells shared, WordPress and reseller hosting, virtual private servers with root access, domain registration and business email. The [VPS page](https://tpc-hosting.com/vps-hosting) names data centers in Germany, Romania, Turkey and the US, offers server management as a paid add-on, and describes a 30-day money-back guarantee. Support runs in English, German and Romanian, starting with an AI assistant on chat.
