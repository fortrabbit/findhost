nine sells the layers separately and lets a customer pick one: managed Kubernetes, a managed server, a root server, or Deploio, its own platform with a build pipeline attached. Databases, object storage, search and caches are sold as managed services beside them, from data centres in Zurich.

## Worth knowing

The interesting part for a buyer outside Switzerland is that everything is in one country and one legal system, which is the argument Swiss hosting has always made and the reason to pay Swiss prices for it.

No price is recorded. What is published is a calculator rather than a plan ladder, and a figure assembled by choosing options is not a starting price this register will quote.
