GreenCloud sells virtual servers: Linux on KVM, Windows on Hyper-V with RDP enabled, and macOS on Apple hardware, which its [macOS VPS page](https://greencloudvps.com/macos-vps.php) pitches for building iOS and Xcode apps. It also sells dedicated servers with Linux or Windows. The [terms of service](https://greencloudvps.com/terms-of-service.php) name 365 Group LLC as the company behind it. The [about page](https://greencloudvps.com/about-us.php) dates the business to October 2013, starting with a location in North Carolina before adding data centers in Europe and Asia.

## Worth knowing

The terms describe the service as unmanaged: support covers hardware and the default operating system installation, not software configuration. Promotional and flash-sale plans are billed on annual or longer cycles and carry their own add-on prices and location-change rules. The seven-day refund covers the first VPS plan only and excludes payments made in cryptocurrency.
