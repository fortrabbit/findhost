United Internet is the German group behind Ionos, 1&1, Fasthosts, Arsys, home.pl and Strato, among others. It is a holding company: nobody buys hosting from United Internet, they buy it from one of its brands.

That is why it is here rather than in the register. The fields this dataset records — runtimes, deployment, regions, price — are properties of a product, and a parent company has none of them. Its brands answer for themselves: [Ionos](/ionos/), [Fasthosts](/fasthosts/), [home.pl](/home-pl/) and [Strato](/strato/) are listed records.

The ownership it represents is recorded where it is decidable, on those records, as [ownership](/ownership/) and a parent. That is the fact worth having: two brands that look independent can answer to the same board, and a price rise or an end of life is decided there.
