Niagahoster was Indonesia's most visible hosting brand, based in Yogyakarta and long the default recommendation in Indonesian web-development circles.

**Criterion 2 — sold to the public — fails, because the brand no longer sells its own product.** Its hosting pages now redirect to hostinger.com's Indonesian storefront. There is no Niagahoster price list, no Niagahoster documentation and no Niagahoster signup remaining; what is sold under that traffic is Hostinger's platform.

Recorded rather than dropped because Niagahoster is still cited across Indonesian tutorials and comparisons, and a reader arriving from one of them should be told what happened. **Hostinger is already listed.**

This is worth noting as a pattern rather than a one-off: consolidation is steadily removing exactly the national providers this dataset exists to cover, and the redirect is usually the only public announcement.
