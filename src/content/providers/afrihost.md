Afrihost is a South African ISP whose headline products are fibre, wireless, mobile and VoIP, with shared hosting as one line among many. Linux plans support PHP, Perl, MySQL, PostgreSQL and CGI, the help centre documents changing PHP version through cPanel, and billing is month-to-month with no contract.

The shape of the company explains some of this: a consumer ISP with a hosting line attracts customers rather than integrators, and the help centre is oriented at panel tasks rather than at developers — no Node.js or Python support is documented at all.
