maxcluster is a Paderborn company selling managed clusters for Magento, Shopware and other e-commerce stacks, with a strong presence in the German shop-development scene.

**Not listed yet, and the open question is the price.** The site sells through a configurator and a quote form rather than a plan table, so whether criterion 3 — a price published without login — is satisfied depends on whether the configurator shows a figure before an account exists. That needs a browser and five minutes. Everything else about it passes: Shopware, Magento, Hyvä, Akeneo, Pimcore, TYPO3 and WordPress on PHP 8 with NGINX, Varnish, Redis, Solr and Elasticsearch, out of its own data centre in Paderborn, billed by the day and cancellable monthly.
