## About Squarespace

Squarespace offers an all-in-one site builder with design-heavy templates, integrated content management, commerce features and AI-assisted site generation. Founded in New York in 2004, the company went private in October 2024 when investment firm Permira completed its acquisition; founder Anthony Casalena stays on as CEO. Squarespace is aimed at creators and small businesses that want a polished site without touching code. The template canvas keeps sites consistent and easy to maintain, but backend access, custom integrations and code portability stay out of reach.
