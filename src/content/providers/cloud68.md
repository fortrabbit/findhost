Cloud68.co provides managed hosting for open-source applications — Nextcloud, Mattermost, WordPress, Mastodon, Vaultwarden and others — for small and medium teams, with a stated open-source and privacy mission and support staffed on Berlin hours.

**Criterion 3 — public pricing — could not be satisfied.** No price for any plan appears on the homepage or the service pages reached from it; visitors are asked to get in touch or book a consultation. Blog posts from July 2026 refer to pricing changes without publishing the figures.

This is the profile of provider the dataset most wants to include — small, independent, European, mission-driven, absent from every commercial comparison — which makes it worth stating plainly that the rejection is mechanical rather than a judgement. **A published rate for any single managed application would be enough to list it.**

Server regions are also not stated anywhere reached this session, so criterion 4 would need checking too.
