## About 20i

20i is a hosting company in Mansfield, Nottinghamshire, registered in England and Wales, running on renewable energy. Its technical distinction is that shared sites are not tied to a single machine: hosting autoscales across a pool of servers, which removes the failure mode where one overloaded box takes a hundred sites with it.

Administration is through My20i, the company's own control panel rather than cPanel, with PHP-FPM tuned in-house, OPCache, per-site PHP version switching, MySQL and phpMyAdmin. Six plans run from £10 a month for a single site to £300 for fifty, and the first month of any of them is £1. Locations are London, Dallas and Singapore, with more available on the managed cloud servers.

## Reservations

The price ladder is steep by British standards — twenty pounds a month for one website on the second tier — and the £1 first month makes the entry figure useless for comparison. SSH availability is not stated anywhere on the hosting page. The proprietary panel is a real switching cost: a site built around My20i does not move to a cPanel host by copying a backup.
