## About 20i

20i is a hosting company registered in England and Wales, operating from Mansfield in Nottinghamshire. Its technical distinction is that shared sites are not tied to a single machine: hosting autoscales across a pool of servers, so no one site is bound to the fortunes of one box. The company matches its power consumption with renewable energy on an annual basis.

Administration runs through My20i, 20i's own control panel rather than cPanel, with per-site PHP version switching, MySQL and phpMyAdmin. Shared plans step up by the number of websites they cover, and a managed cloud server line sits above them with a wider choice of locations.
