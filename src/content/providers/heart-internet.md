A British host aimed at resellers, and part of the Host Europe Group that [GoDaddy](/godaddy/) bought in 2017. GoDaddy sold it on to Your.Online in March 2024, together with the lease on its Leeds data center, so it is no longer a GoDaddy brand. A stub until its hosting and its current owner are recorded.
