WebPros is the group that owns the two control panels most shared hosting is administered through, [cPanel](/cpanel/) and [Plesk](/plesk/), together with the billing system WHMCS, the VPS manager SolusVM and a set of website-building, SEO, social-media, backup and monitoring products, as its [solutions page](https://www.webpros.com/solutions/) lists. The group is run from WebPros International GmbH in Schaffhausen, Switzerland, and its [privacy policy](https://www.webpros.com/legal/) names a separate operating entity for each brand: the Swiss company for Plesk, a US company for cPanel and WHM, a UK company for WHMCS. Nearly everything it sells is licensed to hosting providers, who put their own name on it.

The group was assembled by a private-equity buyer. [Oakley Capital](/holdings/oakley-capital/) carved Plesk out of the Parallels group in 2017, as its [acquisition announcement](https://www.oakleycapital.com/news-and-insights/acquisition-of-plesk) records, and in 2018 bought SolusVM and a majority of cPanel from its founder, per its [cPanel announcement](https://www.oakleycapital.com/news-and-insights/oakley-capital-to-invest-in-cpanel). In December 2019 Oakley agreed to sell the group to a CVC Capital Partners fund while reinvesting as a minority partner, as its [sale announcement](https://www.oakleycapital.com/news-and-insights/oakley-capital-agrees-sale-of-webpros-to-cvc-fund-vii-and-follow-on-investment) sets out, so [CVC](/holdings/cvc/) has been the majority owner since. Two founders of Host Europe, Thomas Strohe and Jochen Berger, invested alongside Oakley from the Plesk deal onward, as Oakley's [case study](https://www.oakleycapital.com/news-and-insights/building-a-business-the-webpros-story) describes; their Cologne investment company, Equivia, lists WebPros on its [portfolio page](https://www.equivia.de/en/portfolio) as a 2017 investment with a partial exit to CVC.

## Worth knowing

WebPros also hosts. [WebPros Cloud](https://www.webpros.cloud/) runs managed WordPress and cPanel shared hosting on infrastructure the group operates, sold to hosting providers, registrars and agencies to resell under their own brand at their own price; its FAQ says a new partner signs a cPanel partner agreement first. Nothing on it is sold to a site owner directly and no price is published, which is why the group sits beside the register while the panels it owns have records in it.
