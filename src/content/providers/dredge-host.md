Dredge Host is a sole proprietorship in New York, started in 2026, that sells cPanel shared hosting on a managed platform in a Virginia data center. Its three plans differ by the number of sites, storage, transfer, mailboxes and databases, each with a published cap, and the [pricing page](https://dredgehost.com/pricing.html) states that a plan renews at the price it was bought at. WordPress installs through Softaculous, and domains are registered through a partner registrar.

## Worth knowing

Support is by email only, seven days a week, with reply times that shorten on the larger plans, as the [contact page](https://dredgehost.com/contact.html) lists. Automated backups are a paid add-on; without it, backups are the manual downloads cPanel offers.
