one.com is a Danish mass-market host selling shared hosting, WordPress hosting and domains across most of Europe, and one of the larger consumer hosting brands on the continent.

The plan ladder runs from a starter tier through managed WordPress to managed VPS, with an AI site builder alongside — the shape of a company selling to people who want a website rather than to people who want a server. Prices are published per plan and per currency, and rendered in the browser, so they are readable by a customer and not by a crawler.

Price bands are not recorded here for that reason. Somebody has to read them with a browser open, and until then the honest answer is unknown.
