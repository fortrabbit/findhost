Servebolt is a Norwegian managed host for PHP applications, sold on page-load and database speed rather than on capacity. It runs WordPress, Drupal, Laravel and Magento among others, deployed by git push, with a CDN and automated backups included and chat support staffed around the clock.

The customers it is shaped for are agencies and in-house teams rather than individuals: the entry plan starts well above the shared-hosting bracket and the range runs up to negotiated agreements.

## Worth knowing

Each plan caps how many live sites it will hold, so what is bought is a bounded set of sites rather than open capacity. An agency outgrows a plan by taking on clients rather than by gaining traffic.

The pricing page does not say where the data centers are. It refers to cloud regions and to renewable power without naming locations for either.
