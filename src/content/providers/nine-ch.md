Nine Internet Solutions sells the layers separately and lets a customer pick one: managed Kubernetes, a managed server, a root server. Databases, object storage, search and caches are sold as managed services beside them, from data centres in Zurich.

## Worth knowing

The argument for a buyer outside Switzerland is that everything sits in one country under one legal system, which is what Swiss hosting has always sold and the reason to pay Swiss prices for it.

No price is recorded. What is published is a calculator rather than a plan ladder, and a figure assembled by choosing options is not a starting price this register will quote.

The company's platform product is recorded separately as [Deploio](/deplo-io/), because pushing a repository and being handed a server are different purchases.
