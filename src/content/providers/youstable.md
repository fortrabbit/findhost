YouStable is the hosting brand of QLOUDIN Technologies Pvt. Ltd., based in Lucknow, India, and has sold hosting since 2015. It sells cPanel shared and WordPress hosting on LiteSpeed, Node.js and Python hosting, Linux and Windows VPS with root or admin access, and dedicated servers. The [data center page](https://www.youstable.com/data-center) lists locations in India, the Netherlands and the US. Prices are published in rupees and US dollars.

## Worth knowing

The [refund policy](https://www.youstable.com/refund-policy) gives thirty days on new shared, WordPress and reseller hosting only; renewals, upgrades and domains are excluded. The [terms](https://www.youstable.com/terms-condition) let the company move an account to another data center without notice and raise prices when license costs change, with fifteen days' notice.
