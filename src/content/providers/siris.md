A US private-equity firm, recorded here because it is one of the two owners of [Newfold Digital](/newfold-digital/), the group that holds Bluehost, HostGator and Network Solutions. Siris held Web.com, which was combined in 2021 with the Endurance International business [Clearlake](/clearlake/) had bought, as the group's [formation announcement](https://www.newfold.com/newsroom/clearlake-completes-acquisition-of-endurance-international-group) records; a December 2025 [financing release](https://www.newfold.com/newsroom/newfold-digital-secures--100-million-investment-to-accelerate-gr) names both firms as its backers still. It takes control-oriented stakes in technology and technology-enabled services companies, as its [own description](https://www.siris.com/) puts it, and sells no hosting under its own name.
