Simafri is a trade name of Sels de Rehy, LLC, a limited liability company incorporated in Delaware, as its [legal notice](https://www.simafri.com/en/legal-notice/) states. It sells virtual servers with root access in fixed sizes, billed monthly or yearly, with nightly encrypted backups kept off the machine for seven days, as the [VPS Pro page](https://www.simafri.com/en/vps/) describes. The same page says technical assistance is included: the team diagnoses a problem and tells the customer what to do, while work done on the server itself is billed by the hour.

A [managed range](https://www.simafri.com/en/managed-vps/) runs the server for the customer at a higher price per size. The [web hosting plans](https://www.simafri.com/en/web-hosting/) cover WordPress sites, PHP and Node.js applications and mailboxes, sold in three plans.
