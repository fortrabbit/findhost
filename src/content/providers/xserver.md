## About Xserver

Xserver is one of the names that dominates Japanese web hosting, in a market that still calls the product a "rental server" and largely runs on its own conventions rather than on the cPanel-and-dollar norms of the anglophone web.

Three plans — Standard, Premium and Business — differ on resources rather than capability, with WordPress tooling, automatic backups, free SSL and a free domain included across all of them. Prices are quoted inclusive of tax and fall as the contract lengthens: the entry plan is around ¥1,100 a month on a twelve-month term and ¥990 on thirty-six, with cashback offers that reduce the effective rate substantially.

## Reservations

Pricing is built around long commitments. The advertised monthly figure assumes a multi-year contract, and the cashback headline rate assumes both the contract and the offer — three different numbers describe the same plan depending on which page you read.

The pricing page states no PHP versions, no SSH availability, no database detail and no data-centre location, so a developer evaluating the platform cannot do it from the page that sells it.
