## About Kinsta

Kinsta is a premium managed WordPress host, founded in 2013. Sites run on Google Cloud's premium-tier network with a Cloudflare-backed CDN and a wide choice of data center regions. The pitch is speed and a polished, fully managed experience: server-level caching, staging, SSH, WP-CLI, and 24/7 support.

Kinsta used to offer general application, database, and static-site hosting too. In early 2026 those non-WordPress products were split off into a separate brand, Sevalla, leaving Kinsta.com focused on WordPress alone. WordPress plans are priced in visit-based tiers starting around $35 a month, and — like other managed WordPress hosts — Kinsta keeps a banned-plugins list for caching, backup, and migration plugins that conflict with its architecture.
