Neubox has operated since 2004 and is one of Mexico's larger .MX registrars, reporting over 213,000 domains under management. Hosting is cPanel shared hosting with a PHP selector reaching 8.3, sold in pesos with OXXO Pay at checkout.

Worth recording: despite strongly Mexican positioning, the stated Tier III infrastructure is in Dallas, Texas.
