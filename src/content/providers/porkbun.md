Porkbun is an ICANN-accredited registrar based in Oregon, selling domains at prices it publishes on the front page and comparing them with what the same name costs elsewhere. Around the registration it has assembled the things a domain tends to need next: mailboxes priced by the inbox, static hosting, a PHP tier, cPanel shared hosting and a site builder.

## Worth knowing

The hosting is the second thing in the basket rather than the product being sold, and its prices are not on the pages the domain search leads to. What is published, prominently, is the domain price and the renewal price beside it — which is not the industry norm and is most of the reason the name is known.
