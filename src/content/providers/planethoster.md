PlanetHoster is a Franco-Canadian host operating data centres in Paris, Lausanne and Montreal, letting a customer pick the location per project — unusual at its price point. It sells "Web Projects" rather than accounts, on its own N0C panel, with PHP 5.6 to 8.5, Python, Node.js and Ruby documented and a public knowledge base covering SSH, Composer, cron and databases. Criteria 1 to 5 pass cleanly.
