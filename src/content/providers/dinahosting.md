## About Dinahosting

Dinahosting has traded from Santiago de Compostela since 2001 and is one of the few Spanish hosts that publishes its runtime matrix rather than leaving it to be discovered. PHP runs from 7.4 through 8.5 with per-site selection; Python, Ruby, Node.js and Perl are supported on Linux and .NET on the Windows plans. Non-root SSH is available from the second tier upwards.

Everything is hosted in Spain with Spanish IP addresses, which matters to customers who need it for search or for compliance reasons, and the company holds green-hosting accreditation for running on renewable energy. Technical support is staffed around the clock. The plan ladder is short — an email-only tier, two hosting tiers and a multihosting plan giving five independent control panels for agencies handling separate clients.

## Reservations

The advertised prices are first-year promotional rates and the standing rates are higher, so the published figure is not what a second-year customer pays. Databases are capped both in number and in size on the lower tiers — one database of one gigabyte on the entry plan — which is a limit that bites before storage does. SSH is not available on the cheapest tier and is never root.
