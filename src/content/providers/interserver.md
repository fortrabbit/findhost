## About InterServer

InterServer is a New Jersey company that owns its data centres and sells one shared hosting plan rather than a ladder of them: unlimited storage, transfer, email and FTP accounts, on DirectAdmin over CloudLinux, with 461 one-click installers, weekly backups, an in-house security system and a free Cloudflare CDN.

Its distinguishing commercial promise is a price lock — the rate a customer signs up at is undertaken not to rise on renewal, which is close to unique in an industry where the introductory-then-double pattern is the norm. The first month is discounted; after that the monthly rate stands, and longer terms are simple multiples of it rather than deeper discounts.

## Reservations

The lock applies to the standing rate, not to the introductory month, so the first bill and the second are still different numbers. PHP is the only documented runtime, and neither SSH availability nor the database options are stated on the plan page. Data-centre locations are named nowhere on it either.
