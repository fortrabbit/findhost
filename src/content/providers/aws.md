## About AWS

Why not host your websites directly on AWS?

AWS provides 100+ services with gazillions of options. You have to invest time to understand and master it. Then you'll need a spreadsheet to calculate your costs. Some parts are managed, but eventually it's your responsibility to maintain and secure it.
