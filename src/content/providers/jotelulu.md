`jotelulu.com` now redirects to `plenit.com`. Per Plenit's own [about page](https://plenit.com/about), the company launched in Spain in 2020 as Jotelulu, selling cloud servers, storage and disaster recovery, and rebranded as Plenit in 2026 after adding IT management tools and a marketplace for reselling Microsoft 365 and other software. The platform is now built explicitly for managed service providers, MSSPs, value-added resellers and cloud service providers to sell on to their own clients, with white-label quoting and billing tools for that purpose.

**Criterion 2 — sold to the public — fails.** Plenit's [pricing page](https://plenit.com/pricing) states that pricing "is not publicly available because the platform operates under a wholesale model exclusively for the IT sector channel," and is "shared privately with verified partners once onboarding is complete." The only calls to action on the marketing site are to request a demo or contact sales; there is no self-service signup for an end customer.

**This reverses if Plenit, or a successor selling the same infrastructure, opens direct signup and public pricing to end customers again.**
