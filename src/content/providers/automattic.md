Automattic is the company around WordPress: it runs WordPress.com, owns [Pressable](/pressable/), and publishes WooCommerce, Jetpack and much else that the wider WordPress economy is built on.

Nobody buys hosting from Automattic. The hosting reaches customers under the brand names, which is why this record is here without being in the register — it is the owner above two entries rather than a third one beside them.
