Ownership is recorded because it shapes the things a customer eventually meets: how prices move, how long a product line survives, how support is staffed, and what happens after an acquisition. An independent company and a private-equity portfolio company can offer identical plans today and behave very differently in three years.

It is a field to apply a preference to, never a recommendation this site makes. Independence is not a guarantee of anything, and being publicly listed is not a criticism — the largest and most reliable providers in the dataset are listed companies. Some readers care about this a great deal and some not at all, which is exactly why it is a filter rather than a verdict.
