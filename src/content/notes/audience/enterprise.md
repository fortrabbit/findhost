At this end the technology is rarely the deciding factor. What is bought is a contract: an SLA with a number in it, a DPA and sub-processor list that survives a review, invoicing that fits a purchase order, and an escalation path with a name attached. Providers selling here often do not publish prices, which is itself the signal — and it is why [entry price](/entry-price/) is missing on many of these records.
