Startup hosting is chosen twice: once when nobody is using the product and cost is the only criterion, and again when something works and the first choice starts creaking. Providers marketing here usually offer credits at the start and a path upwards that does not involve a migration. The risk to read carefully is [billing in arrears](/pricing/in-arrears/), which is generous until traffic arrives unannounced.
