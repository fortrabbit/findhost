---

Non-profit hosting is usually the ordinary product with a discount attached, but the operating reality differs: the site is maintained by volunteers, in gaps, with no handover between them. That makes updates applied by the provider and a restore that works without an expert worth more than capacity. Check whether the discount survives renewal, which is a [pricing](/pricing/) question rather than a charitable one.
