Universities and schools host in two shapes: a lot of small sites and accounts for students and departments, and occasional research workloads that are large but brief. Both are price-sensitive and neither fits a standard business plan, which is why providers selling here usually offer academic terms. Long-lived project sites outliving the person who made them are the norm rather than the exception.
