A small business runs its site as infrastructure rather than as a project: it takes bookings or orders, nobody on staff maintains it, and the agency that built it may be long gone. That puts the weight on support hours, an invoice the accountant accepts, and the boring reliability of a provider that will still be there in five years — [ownership](/ownership/) and founding year are the fields for the last of those.
