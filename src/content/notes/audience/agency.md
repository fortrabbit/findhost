An agency hosts on behalf of clients, which adds requirements a solo account never meets: several sites under one login, team members who come and go, invoices a client can be shown, and eventually a clean way to hand a site over. Providers selling to agencies usually mean a reseller arrangement or a team plan, and the handover path is worth checking before the relationship ends rather than after.
