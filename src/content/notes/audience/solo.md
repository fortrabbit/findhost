A freelancer is a small business. The hosting is priced accordingly, billed on a card, often self-service, documented well enough that nobody has to open a ticket at midnight. Good web hosting providers support freelancers with no sales call, no annual commitments, collaboration features that support to invite a client and some free time to pre-launch a project.
