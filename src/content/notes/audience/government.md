Public sector buying is governed by frameworks, tender processes and rules about where data may sit, which narrows the field before anyone compares features. Providers selling here can answer questions about jurisdiction, sub-processors and certification in writing. Where the servers are is only half of it — [who owns the company](/ownership/) and whose hardware it runs on decide who can be compelled to hand data over.
