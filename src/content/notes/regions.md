faq:
  - q: 'Does hosting in {label} decide which law applies?'
    a: 'No. The region is where the servers run. The contract is with a company, and the country it is [based in](/headquarters/) decides which courts and authorities can reach the data. A provider from elsewhere can run servers in {label}. For personal data, check the data processing agreement too.'
---

A record names countries, so a dot marks a country's principal hosting metro rather than a data center, and a country with several hosting regions spreads its dot between them. Every country a listed provider names is on the list below, each leading to its own page; a provider that [brings its own infrastructure](/metal/byo-iaas/) has no regions to name. Where the company behind the racks is registered is a [separate list](/headquarters/).
