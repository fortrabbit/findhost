A record names countries, so a dot marks a country's principal hosting metro rather than a data center, and a country with several hosting regions spreads its dot between them. Every country a listed provider names is on the list below, each leading to its own page; a provider that [brings its own infrastructure](/metal/byo-iaas/) has no regions to name.
