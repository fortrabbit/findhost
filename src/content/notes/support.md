description: The ways in when something breaks — email or ticket, chat, a phone number, a community forum.
lead: How to reach someone, and whether that someone works there.
---

This records the channels a provider publishes, not how good the help is. Response times are not published by anyone and could only be asserted here, which is the one thing this dataset will not do — so what is recorded is the checkable part: whether a route exists at all. A phone number is the rarest of them and the most telling, because staffing one costs money in a way a help center does not. A **forum** is the opposite end: real support, sometimes excellent, but the people answering do not work there and owe nobody a reply.

Two related fields sit beside it on a record and are not filterable: **hours**, which is when a human can be reached about a production problem on the cheapest paid plan, and **tiering**, which is whether the answer changes with what is being paid. Most records do not carry any of the three yet — an unfilled field says so rather than guessing.
