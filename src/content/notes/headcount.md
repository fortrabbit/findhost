title: How many people are behind a host
description: Hosting providers by the number of people who work there, in bands, from what each company says about itself.
lead: Three people, or three thousand.
---

Everything else in this register describes the product; this describes the company, and it is the question least likely to be answered on a pricing page. Neither direction is better. Six people means support from whoever wrote the platform, and no company at all if two of them leave. Six hundred means a rota, an escalation path, and a reply from someone who has never seen the code. Bands rather than numbers, taken from what a company says about itself — an about page, a team page, an imprint. Silence stays unknown, and nothing here ranks by it.
