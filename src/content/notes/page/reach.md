Networks linking to each provider's own domain, from the [Majestic Million](https://majestic.com/reports/majestic-million), CC BY 3.0. The bars are logarithmic because the largest figure is about a thousand times the smallest; the linear scale shows the range as it is. It measures how linked-to a domain is, not how good or how big a host is.
