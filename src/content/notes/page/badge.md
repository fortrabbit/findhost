Every provider in the register is welcome to take this and put it on their own site. It says one thing — that there is a record here — and it says the same thing for all of them, because the alternative is the market this project exists to be an alternative to: seals that imply a ranking, sold by the year. The artwork loads nothing from this server, carries no tracking parameter, and works the same whether it is displayed or not.
