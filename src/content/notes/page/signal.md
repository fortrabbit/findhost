Most of this register is deliberately flat. Providers are listed alphabetically, described by what they record, and nothing is scored — because a number attached to a hosting provider is usually somebody's commission wearing a lab coat.

This is the one place we say what we think. A great many hosting companies sell the same product as each other: a control panel, a PHP version, a price that triples on renewal, and nothing that has changed in a decade. That is the noise. The signal is the other kind — platforms doing something that was not obvious, run as businesses that can still be here in five years, by people who took a position instead of a market share.

So the ordering rewards what we would want ourselves. Independence over ownership by a group. A platform you can drive from a script rather than only from a browser. Somewhere to try a deploy before it is the site. Running an application, rather than renting a machine and wishing you luck.

It is our taste, and we are in it — fortrabbit publishes this register and appears in the list it orders. That is why the whole of the arithmetic is on this page rather than described on it: disagree with a weight, and you are disagreeing with something you can read.
