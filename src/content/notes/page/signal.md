The register is alphabetical everywhere else, and nothing in it is scored. This is the one place we say what we think.

A great many hosting companies sell the same product as each other: a control panel, a PHP version, a price that triples on renewal, nothing changed in a decade. That is the noise. The signal is platforms doing something that was not obvious, run as businesses that can still be here in five years.

So the ordering rewards independence, a platform you can drive from a script, somewhere to try a deploy before it is the site. It is our taste, and we are in it — fortrabbit publishes this register and appears in the list it orders. Which is why the arithmetic is printed below rather than described: disagree with a weight, and you are disagreeing with something you can read.

A weight starts counting once a fifth of the register answers the field it reads, so a good idea nobody has researched yet cannot decide the order. Where the arithmetic reads wrong, a person may move one record by hand, with a name, a date and a reason — those are listed at the foot of this page.
