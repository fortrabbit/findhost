## How the signal works

Two things make the order, and the split matters.

**Points, from facts already recorded** on each provider's page — the table below is the whole of it, and it is generated from the code that runs, so it cannot drift from what actually happened. A weight starts counting only once a fifth of the register answers the field it reads, which keeps a good idea nobody has researched yet from deciding anything.

**And our own hand.** The fields cannot see whether a company is genuinely trying something, deals straight, or is built for people who write code, so where that matters somebody moves the record — with a name, a date and a reason, every one of them listed at the foot of this page. Today that is doing a lot of the work. We would rather more of it came from the data, and finding out how much can is part of why this exists.
