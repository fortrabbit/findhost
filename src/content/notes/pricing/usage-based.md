lead: No plan, only consumption.
---

Metered pricing removes the plan entirely and charges for what happened: requests served, data transferred, seconds of compute. It suits genuinely spiky work and it is the hardest bill to predict from outside, because the price depends on traffic nobody has seen yet. Comparing it against a fixed plan requires an estimate, and the estimate is usually where the surprise lives.
