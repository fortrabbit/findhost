---
description: Hosting with a free trial — free access that ends on a date or at a quota, rather than continuing.
lead: Free until it is not.
---

A trial is time-boxed or quota-boxed access to the real product, which makes it more informative than a free tier and less useful for anything that has to keep running. The questions worth asking are whether a card is required to start and what happens at the end — a trial that converts silently into a paid plan is a common and legal arrangement.
