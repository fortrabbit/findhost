It is about leaving, not about joining — nothing here is required to sign up, and this says nothing is required to stop either. No minimum term means the arrangement ends when it is ended: no notice period, no remaining months, no prepaid year to write off. It is the most reversible way to buy hosting and it usually costs a little more than committing, which is the trade. How long a bill outlives the decision to stop is recorded separately as [exit](/exit/).
