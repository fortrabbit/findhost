description: Hosting with no minimum term — leaving costs nothing but the decision.
lead: Cancel and that is the end of it.
---

No minimum term means the arrangement ends when it is ended: no notice period, no remaining months, no prepaid year to write off. It is the most reversible way to buy hosting and it usually costs a little more than committing, which is the trade. How long a bill outlives the decision to stop is recorded separately as [exit](/exit/).
