It is about leaving, not about joining — nothing here is required to sign up, and this says nothing is required to stop either. No minimum term means nothing is owed for time not yet served: no remaining months, no prepaid year to write off. It is not a promise that the bill stops the same day — how long that takes is a separate question, recorded as [exit](/exit/). It is the most reversible way to buy hosting and it usually costs a little more than committing, which is the trade.
