A permanent free tier means something runs indefinitely without a card being charged: a small instance, a static site, a database under a quota. It is a marketing instrument as much as a product, and the free tier is frequently a different product from the paid one wearing the same name. What it settles well is whether an application actually runs; what it says about the paid plans is nothing.
