description: Hosting billed in arrears — the meter runs first and the invoice follows, so the ceiling is whatever happened.
lead: The bill is a consequence.
---

Billing in arrears means paying for what was used after using it. Nobody pays for idle capacity, which is the appeal, and nobody knows the number in advance, which is the risk: a loop that ran all weekend, a bot that found an expensive endpoint, or a backup copying more than expected all arrive as an invoice rather than as a limit. Spending caps, where a provider offers them, are the thing to look for.
