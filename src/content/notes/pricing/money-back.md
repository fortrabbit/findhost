A money-back window is a promise to undo a charge that has already been made, on terms the provider writes and by a process it runs, and the terms are where it thins out: domains and third-party licenses are almost always excluded, monthly plans often are, and the refund is usually claimed through the same support queue as everything else. Thirty days is the shared-hosting habit that most of the market copied. A [free trial](/pricing/free-trial/) answers the same worry earlier by never taking the money, which is why the window is recorded here and the exact number of days is left on the record.
