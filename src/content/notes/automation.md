**How much ClickOps in the provider's dashboard, how much can be automated and prompted?** An **API** makes the account programmable at all; a **CLI** is the same capability shaped for a terminal and for scripts, an **MCP** server exposes the platform to language models directly, which is new enough that very few hosting providers have one.
