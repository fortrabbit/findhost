description: Which hosting providers can be driven by something other than a browser — a public API, a command-line tool, or an MCP server.
---

Three ways in, and a provider can offer any combination. An **API** makes the account programmable at all; a **CLI** is the same capability shaped for a terminal and for scripts, and is usually what a deployment pipeline reaches for; an **MCP** server exposes the platform to language models directly, which is new enough that very few hosting providers have one. The facet is derived rather than recorded, so the detail — official or third-party, public or partner-only — stays on the provider's own page, where the distinction still means something.
