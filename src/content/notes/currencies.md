---
description: The currencies a provider will actually bill in, which decides who carries the conversion.
---

Every provider quotes a price in something, and the something matters more than it looks. A European business billed in dollars pays the exchange rate on the day plus whatever the card issuer adds for a foreign transaction, and neither figure appears anywhere on the pricing page. The same plan therefore costs a different amount depending on where the buyer banks, and it moves month to month.

Recorded as the currencies a provider will invoice in, not the ones a website can display. A price page with a currency switcher that still charges in dollars at checkout is charging in dollars. Where a provider bills in the local currency of the market it sells to, that is worth knowing next to [where it operates](/regions/) and where it is headquartered — the money is the other half of how local a host really is.
