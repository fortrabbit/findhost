---
description: The currencies a hosting provider will actually bill in, which decides who carries the conversion.
---

Every provider quotes a price in something, and the something matters more than it looks: a European business billed in dollars pays the exchange rate on the day plus whatever the card issuer adds for a foreign transaction, and neither figure appears on the pricing page. The same plan therefore costs a different amount depending on where the buyer banks, and it moves month to month. Recorded here are the currencies a provider will invoice in, not the ones a website can display — a price page with a currency switcher that still charges in dollars at checkout is charging in dollars.
