lead: What the provider says it is for.
---

Every value here is a claim taken from a provider's own marketing, never a judgement about what it is good at. A host that puts online shops on its front page is recorded as marketing itself for online shops, whether or not it is the best place to run one.

Most providers claim several, and the overlap is the point: it shows which corners of the market a platform is trying to occupy. What a provider is *used* for is a different question, and one this dataset cannot answer honestly — nobody publishes that. Who it sells to is [audience](/audience/), which is kept separate because a side project and a government department can want the same software and will never want the same contract.
