A web hosting provider that puts online shops on its front page is recorded as marketing itself for online shops, whether or not it is the best place to run one. Most providers claim several use cases. Who it sells to is [audience](/audience/), which is kept separate because a side project and a government department can want the same software and will never want the same contract.
