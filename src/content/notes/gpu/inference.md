---
description: Hosting providers that serve your own or Hugging Face model weights from a managed endpoint.
---

Hand over the weights, from your own storage or the Hugging Face hub, and get back an HTTPS endpoint. The provider picks the serving stack, scales the replicas and keeps the card busy; the model stays yours. A token API over a catalogue the provider chose is a different product, and is shown on the record rather than listed here.
