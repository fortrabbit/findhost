description: Hosting providers that rent out servers with a GPU in them, virtual or dedicated, by the hour or by the month.
---

A server with a card in it. The provider racks the hardware and installs the driver; the framework, the model and the process serving it are yours, as is the bill while the machine sits idle. Some providers sell GPU servers only on request, and the record says so where the provider does. Who manages the rest of the stack is under [operating system](/operating-system/).
