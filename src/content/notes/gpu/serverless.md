---
description: Hosting providers that run your own container on a GPU only while it has work, billed by the second or minute and scaled to zero.
---

Your container, their GPU, and nothing on the bill while no request is running. The trade is the cold start: the first request after a quiet spell waits for the image to load and the weights to reach the card. Providers that sell the same thing over their own models rather than yours are recorded as a model API and not listed here.
