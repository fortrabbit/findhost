---
title: Domain included
description: Hosting plans that come with a domain name at no extra charge, as against hosts that sell you one separately.
lead: A domain in the price, not on the invoice.
---

Shared hosts often throw a name in, sometimes only for the first year; application platforms rarely register anything and expect the domain to arrive from elsewhere. This counts only where it comes at no extra charge — sold on its own line is an add-on, which is the ordinary answer and not a yes here. It is a fact about a plan, not about a company: a host bundling a free domain is not thereby in the domain business, and the [companies that sell names](/categories/domains-dns/) are a different list, overlapping this one by two records out of twenty.
