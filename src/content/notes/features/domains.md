description: Hosting that registers the domain too, rather than leaving it as a second purchase somewhere else.
lead: One invoice instead of two.
---

A site needs a name before it needs a server, and the two are usually bought from different companies. Shared hosts often throw a domain in, sometimes only for the first year; application platforms rarely register anything at all and expect the name to arrive from elsewhere. Recorded as included only where it comes at no extra charge — sold on its own line is an add-on, which is the ordinary answer.
