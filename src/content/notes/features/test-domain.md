---
description: Hosting that hands out a working URL of its own, so a deploy can be seen before a domain is pointed at it.
lead: A URL before the domain arrives.
---

Something like `myapp.provider.app`, given out with the account and working immediately. It matters more than it sounds: it means a deploy can be checked, shared and reviewed before anyone touches a nameserver, and it is how most platforms show a pull request to the person who opened it. Free wherever it exists at all, which is why this field has no add-on answer.
