description: Hosting that answers for the domain as well as serving the site, with no separate DNS bill.
lead: Somebody has to answer for the name.
---

Registering a domain and hosting its DNS are different jobs, and a provider may do one, both or neither. This records whether the host runs the nameservers itself at no extra charge. Where it does not, the records point somewhere else — the registrar, a dedicated DNS operator, or a network provider — and that is one more account to hold.
