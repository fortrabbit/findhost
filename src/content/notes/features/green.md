---
title: Hosting providers that publish a green energy claim
description: Which hosting providers say something checkable about the energy they run on, what the three kinds of green claim actually mean, and why absent means unknown.
---

Whether a hosting service runs on green energy and/or is offsetting CO2 consumption. We will trust the providers own claim for that and also try to match it with the [Green Web Foundation](https://www.thegreenwebfoundation.org/). Note that [AWS leaving their dataset in March 2025](https://www.thegreenwebfoundation.org/news/an-update-on-finding-representatives-for-large-hosting-providers/) makes every site hosted on it read "not green" whatever the host's own evidence says. Absent means unknown.
