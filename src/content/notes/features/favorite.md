The one editorial mark in the register, and the only place it says what we think rather than what we found. It began as a scoring system that added up favorable features and then let a person move the total by hand; after eighteen adjustments the arithmetic was decoration, so it became a boolean. Each record marked here gives its own one-line reason, and the reasons do not agree with each other — some are innovative, some are small and worth knowing about, some have simply behaved well for a long time. There is no opposite box: "the ones we did not pick" would be a verdict on two hundred companies nobody passed, and absence here means only that nobody chose it. Ticking this changes what is shown and never the order, which stays alphabetical, and [our own service](/fortrabbit/) is in the list — the [bias is disclosed](/about/) rather than removed.
