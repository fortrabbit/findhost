---
description: Hosting that takes up to a quarter to leave — a notice period, or a term that ends on a quarterly boundary.
lead: Three months of runway to go.
---

A quarter usually means a contract with notice rather than a plan with a renewal date, and it is common where hosting is sold with services attached. The practical effect is that leaving has to be planned a season ahead: a decision made in March takes effect in June, and the bill continues in between.
