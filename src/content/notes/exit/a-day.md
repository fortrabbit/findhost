The most reversible end of the register: hourly or monthly billing, cancellation from the account, no notice period and no remainder. It is the norm for cloud infrastructure and for platforms billing by usage, and it is what makes trying something cheap — the cost of a wrong choice is the days it ran, not the year it was booked for.
