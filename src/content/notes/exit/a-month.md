The common shape for hosting paid up front: cancel and the service runs to the end of the period already bought. Nothing is refunded and nothing further is owed. The distinction from [within a day](/exit/a-day/) is what was paid for, not how often the invoice arrives — a month bought in advance is a month committed, and a month billed in arrears is not. It is short enough that the commitment rarely affects a decision, which is why most providers land here and why the bands above it are the ones worth noticing.
