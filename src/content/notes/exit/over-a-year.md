Multi-year terms appear at the consumer end, where a cheap headline price is paid for by a long commitment, and at the enterprise end, where the contract is negotiated and the term is deliberate. In both cases the money keeps going out well past the point of regret, which is why the term belongs beside the price rather than in the small print under it.
