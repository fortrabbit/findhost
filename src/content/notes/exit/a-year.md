lead: A year of committed spending.
---

An annual commitment is bought for the discount and it is real money either way: cancelling early stops the renewal rather than the payment already made. That is a reasonable trade for something known to be needed, and a poor one for anything provisional. It is also where a low [entry price](/entry-price/) often comes from, and the two should be read together.
