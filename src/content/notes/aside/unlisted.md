title: Unlisted
description: Companies that own hosting brands and sell no hosting under their own name. They keep their records; they are not part of the register.
lead: Owns hosting brands, sells none.
---

United Internet sells nobody a server. Ionos, the part of it that does, is in the register with its runtimes, its regions and its prices. The parent has none of those, so the fields here cannot describe it — every column would be blank, and a blank column reads as a fact about a company rather than as a question that does not apply to it.

They keep a page because ownership is one of the things this register is most useful for. Somebody who recognizes a name from a headline should be able to find which brands stand in front of it, and somebody weighing up two familiar brands should be able to find out they are choosing from one company twice. The [ownership](/ownership/) field records the same relation from the other side, on the record of each brand.

A company that sells hosting under its own name as well as owning others is not here — it is in the register, with its parentage recorded like anybody else's. GoDaddy and Ionos both own more brands than most readers would guess, and both sell hosting themselves, so both are listed.
