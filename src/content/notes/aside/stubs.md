Why is that one not in the list? A stub is a name with a handful of fields and no research behind it yet — visible so that the work in progress can be seen, and so that nobody starts the same provider twice. A ruling is a name that was considered and left out against one of the numbered criteria on the [about page](/about/), and it keeps a page because a decision nobody can link to is not a decision. The argument for reversing one starts by reading the criterion it failed.

Neither is counted, and the filters do not reach them. A record with three fields would drop out on the first tick as though somebody had checked and found it wanting, which is the opposite of what an unfinished record means.

Being out of scope is not a criticism. Most of the rulings are reversible, and several are near-misses on a single criterion — usually published pricing. Bring the missing evidence and the record moves into the register.
