Why is that hosting provider not in the list? Every provider starts here. A stub is a provider I have reviewed and not picked for the list yet, or one that fails one of the [five checks](/criteria/) and names the number. Stubs are not counted, and the filters do not reach them. I review them again from time to time, and new evidence moves a provider into the list.
