Why is that hosting provider not in the list? Every provider starts here. A stub has been checked and did not make the full list: it failed one of the [listing criteria](/criteria/), and then names its number, or the review did not pick it. Stubs are not counted, and the filters do not reach them. I check them again from time to time, and new evidence moves a provider into the list.
