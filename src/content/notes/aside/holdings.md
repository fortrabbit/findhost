title: Holdings
description: Companies that own hosting brands and sell no hosting under their own name, recorded so the ownership behind a brand can be followed.
lead: Owns hosting brands, sells none.
---

These companies are neither [defunct](/defunct/) nor [stubs](/stubs/), and they are not in the register either: they own hosting brands and sell no hosting under their own name. They keep a record because ownership is useful to know about, and the [ownership](/ownership/) field records the same relation from the other side, on the record of each brand. A company that sells hosting as well as owning others is not here. It is in the register, with its parentage recorded like anybody else's — GoDaddy and Ionos both own more brands than most readers would guess, and both sell hosting themselves.
