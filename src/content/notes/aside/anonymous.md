---
title: Anonymous hosting
description: Hosts whose own pages sell sign-up without identification, kept beside the register rather than in it.
lead: Sign up with an email address, pay in cryptocurrency, and the host does not know who you are.
---

These hosts advertise anonymity as the product: an account needs nothing but an email address, and payment in cryptocurrency keeps even that loose. They sit beside the register because the same feature serves opposite ends. It is what makes them useful to a journalist or a campaign facing a hostile state, and it means the host does not know who runs anything on its machines, including the neighbours on a shared server. The test is the provider's own wording, quoted on each record: a host that asks for no identification without selling the fact stays in the register. Where the servers stand matters here more than anywhere, so the [regions](/regions/) of each record are worth reading as a legal question.
