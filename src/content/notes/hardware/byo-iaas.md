Here the provider never touches hardware: servers are created in the customer's own cloud account, and what is bought is the tooling that manages them. The bill splits in two, the infrastructure relationship stays with the customer, and the provider can be replaced without moving anything. It is also why some of these records have no [regions](/regions/) of their own to report.
