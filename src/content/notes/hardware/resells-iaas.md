Reselling is an ordinary way to build a platform and several of the best-regarded providers do it: the product is the software, the operations and the support, not the metal. It matters for two reasons. Jurisdiction follows the underlying cloud rather than the brand, and the provider's costs move when its supplier's do. Which upstream is used is recorded on the record where it is known.
