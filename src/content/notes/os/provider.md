Where the provider manages the OS, the kernel and the system packages are updated without anyone asking, and in exchange the runtime versions, the web server configuration and the installed extensions are theirs to decide. It is the defining trait of a platform as opposed to a server, and it usually pairs with a [jailed shell](/shell/jailed/) rather than root.
