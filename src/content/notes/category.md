The hosting market has no clean labels. They shift with new technology, vendors blur them on purpose — [which is most of why choosing is hard](/guide/why-hosting-is-hard/). This page is the map the dataset uses: what each category is. Every record carries exactly one of the eight as its primary category, plus a list of what else it offers, because most providers sell across more than one. Each category has a page of its own: what it is, what it asks of a team, the field that tells it apart from the one next door, and the providers in it. Managed hosting is the ninth term in common use and the one that fits no category at all.
