title: Web hosting categories
description: The categories the market sells under — shared, VPS, server management, vanity hosting, serverless, PaaS, IaaS, low-code — and what each one asks of a team.
---

The hosting market has no clean labels. Providers are trying to find sweet spots and 'disrupt'. Categories shift with new technology, vendors blur them. [Choosing is hard](/guide/). Every record carries one of the eight as its primary category, plus a list of what else it offers, because most providers sell across more than one.
