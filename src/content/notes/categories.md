title: Web hosting categories
description: The categories the market sells under — shared, VPS, bare metal, IaaS, PaaS, CaaS, serverless, static, server management, vanity hosting, low-code — and what each one asks of a team.
---

The hosting market has no clean labels. Providers are trying to find sweet spots and 'disrupt'. Categories shift with new technology, vendors blur them. [Choosing is hard](/guide/). A record carries every category it sells under rather than one chosen as primary, because most providers sell across several and picking one would be picking for them.
