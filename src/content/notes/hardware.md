**Owns the hardware** means racks the company is responsible for. **Resells someone else's** means a layer of software over capacity rented from a larger cloud. **You bring your own** means the product is the automation and the infrastructure arrives from the customer's own account. More than one can be true at once, and often is.

This is the field that most often contradicts a marketing page. Jurisdiction claims in particular depend on it: a platform in one country running on a hyperscaler's region in another is a common shape, and the layer that decides where the data physically sits is one step below anything the marketing describes. Recording it is not an accusation — reselling is a perfectly ordinary way to build a platform, and several of the best-regarded ones do it. It is simply a fact that a reader weighing [regions](/regions/) or ownership needs in order to read the rest correctly.
