---

A phone number is the least common thing in this field and the most revealing, because staffing one costs money in a way a help center does not. It usually indicates a provider selling to businesses that expect to reach someone, and it often appears alongside longer hours and a contract. Its absence is not a criticism — most modern platforms deliberately do not offer it.
