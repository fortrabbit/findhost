Email or a ticket form is the default, offered by nearly every provider, which makes its presence uninformative and its absence telling. What varies is who reads it and how fast, and neither is published by anyone — so this register records that the route exists and stops there. Hours and whether the answer improves on a paid plan sit beside it on each record.
