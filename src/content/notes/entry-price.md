The entry price is what it costs to run a single small production application with everything it needs, in US dollars a month, sorted into seven bands whose boundaries roughly triple. That puts the resolution where the shopping happens: a band covers $5 to $15 at the bottom and $500 to $1,500 at the top, so the cheap end gets as much of the scale as the expensive end. Bands rather than figures, deliberately. Exact prices change without notice, differ by region and currency, and a stale number read as current is worse than an honest range. A free tier is not on this scale at all — "free" describes what a provider gives away, and this describes what it charges. That is recorded under [pricing features](/pricing/), and what it costs to stop paying is [getting out](/exit/).
