The entry price is what it costs to run a single small production application with everything it needs, in US dollars a month, sorted into seven bands whose boundaries roughly triple. Bands rather than figures. Exact prices change without notice, differ by region and currency, and a stale number read as current is worse. A free tier is not on this scale at all — "free" describes what a provider gives away, and this describes what it charges. That is recorded under [pricing features](/pricing/): a [free tier](/pricing/free-tier/) keeps running under a paid plan, a [free trial](/pricing/free-trial/) has a clock on it, and where the free account is the whole product the category is [free hosting](/categories/free-hosting/). What it costs to stop paying is [getting out](/exit/).
