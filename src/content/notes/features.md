description: What comes with the hosting beyond running the code — collaboration, staging, domains, DNS, email, a CDN, a test URL — as against who sells each of them as a business.
---

A hosting plan is rarely the whole job. Domains have to be registered somewhere, DNS served by someone, email run on a machine that is not the one serving the site — most application platforms include none of the three, a shared host often includes all of them. Each is recorded as included, sold as an add-on, or not offered. A record showing nothing here is one nobody has checked, not one with seven noes. Who sells these things as a business is [a category](/categories/domains-dns/) rather than a feature.
