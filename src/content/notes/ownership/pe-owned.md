Private equity buys hosting companies for predictable revenue, and the playbook is well documented: consolidate brands, raise renewal prices, reduce support cost, sell in a few years. The brand, the control panel and the support signature usually stay exactly as they were, which is why the field is worth recording — nothing visible changes, and the terms do.
