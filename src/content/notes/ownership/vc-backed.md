description: Hosting providers with venture capital behind them — growth funded now, with the reckoning scheduled later.
lead: Growth first, on somebody else's money.
---

Venture funding buys generous free tiers, fast product development and prices that do not have to cover costs yet. All of that is real while it lasts, and it is paid for by an expectation of a return: an acquisition, a listing, or a repricing. The pattern to watch for is not failure but success — the plan that made sense at launch is frequently not the plan three funding rounds later.
