A side project is an application with an audience of one to a hundred and no revenue behind it. What it needs is a low floor rather than a high ceiling: something that costs little or nothing while it is idle, deploys without ceremony, and can be abandoned without an invoice arriving afterwards. A [free tier](/pricing/) is aimed squarely here, and so is the risk of billing in arrears.
