A campaign site exists for a launch, an event or a season, and its traffic arrives all at once when something is sent or published. The hosting requirements are the opposite of a long-running application: almost no state, no background work, and enough burst capacity to survive the one hour that matters. Static output behind a CDN covers most of it, and the rest is being able to stop paying afterwards — see [exit](/exit/).
