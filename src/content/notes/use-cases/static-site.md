description: Hosting marketed for static sites — files served as they are, with the build happening somewhere before that.
lead: No runtime to go wrong.
---

Static hosting has nothing to crash: HTML, CSS and images are served from disk or a CDN edge. That makes it the cheapest and most reliable thing to host, and it moves the interesting question to the build — whether the provider runs the site generator on every push, or expects a finished directory. Where a site needs a form or a search, it needs a second service too.
