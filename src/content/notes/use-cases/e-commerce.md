A shop breaks the assumption most web hosting is tuned for. Catalog pages cache well; cart, checkout and account pages cannot, so traffic lands on the application and the database at exactly the moment it matters. Add a search service, a session store, background indexing and PCI questions, and shop hosting becomes its own product. Providers marketing it usually mean sized infrastructure rather than an installer entry.
