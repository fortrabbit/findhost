Learning hosting is judged on how quickly something can be deployed, how little it costs while it does nothing, and how easily it can be thrown away. Documentation matters more than capacity, and a permanent free tier matters more than either. Nothing here needs to survive: the point is the attempt, which is also why [getting out](/exit/) should be trivial.
