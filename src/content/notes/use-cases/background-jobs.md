---
description: Hosting marketed for background jobs and queues — the half of an application that runs after the response.
lead: The work that outlives the request.
---

Sending mail, resizing images, generating reports and calling slow APIs all happen after the browser has been answered, which needs a process that is not the web one. Providers marketing this support a second process type and keep it supervised; providers that do not will run the application and silently drop everything queued. It is the most common thing to discover missing after choosing a host.
