description: Whether domain registration, DNS, email and a CDN come with the hosting or have to be bought somewhere else.
---

A hosting plan is rarely the whole bill. Domains have to be registered somewhere, DNS has to be served by someone, and email usually runs on a machine that is not the one serving the site — most application platforms include none of it, while a shared host often includes all of it. Neither is better; they are different products, and the difference is a second supplier, a second invoice and a second thing to move when leaving. Each is recorded as included, sold as an add-on, or not offered, because "they will sell it separately" is the ordinary answer and a yes-or-no has nowhere to put it. A record showing nothing here is one nobody has checked, not one with three noes.
