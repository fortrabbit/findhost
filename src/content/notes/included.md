description: Whether domain registration, DNS hosting and email come with the hosting or have to be bought somewhere else.
lead: What arrives with the hosting, and what is a second invoice.
---

A hosting plan is rarely the whole bill. Domains have to be registered somewhere, DNS has to be served by someone, and email has to run on a machine that is not usually the one serving the site — most application platforms include none of the three, while a shared host often includes all of them. Neither is better; they are different products, and the difference is a second supplier, a second invoice and a second thing to move when leaving.

The three are recorded as yes and no rather than as a list of what is included, because a list can only say the first. Absent still means nobody has checked, which is why a record showing nothing here is not a record showing three noes.
