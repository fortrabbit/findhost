---
title: Where providers are based
description: The country each hosting company is registered in, which is a different question from where it runs servers.
---

A headquarters is where the company is rather than where the hardware is, and the two come apart often: a contract is signed with a legal entity and a court order is served on one, so the country behind the invoice decides whose law applies and which authority can compel a disclosure. A provider based in one country may [operate in](/regions/) a dozen, and some operate in none of their own because they resell another company's racks. Recorded from the provider's own pages, an imprint, an about page or the terms, and left absent where none of them says.
