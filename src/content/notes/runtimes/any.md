"Anything" means arbitrary processes, which in practice means a server with root rather than a platform with a supported runtime list. The language question stops being interesting and the operational one starts: somebody has to install the runtime, patch it and keep it running, and [who manages the operating system](/operating-system/) is the field that says who. Providers in this group are usually recorded under VPS or bare metal as well.
