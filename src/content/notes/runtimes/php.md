PHP is supported almost everywhere, which makes its presence the least interesting thing about a host and its version policy the most. The questions worth asking are how quickly new releases arrive, how long old ones keep receiving them, and whether an upgrade is a switch or a migration. Composer support and a writable deployment path matter more in practice than the language badge — a host can run PHP and still make a modern framework awkward to deploy. Nearly everything in [software](/software/) sits on top of it: [WordPress](/software/wordpress/) and [Drupal](/software/drupal/), [Laravel](/software/laravel/) and [Symfony](/software/symfony/), [Craft CMS](/software/craft-cms/), [Kirby](/software/kirby/) and [Magento](/software/magento/).
