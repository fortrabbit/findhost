Deno is documented by few hosting providers, and its own platform is the obvious home for it. Elsewhere a container image is the practical route: the runtime is a single binary and the permission model is the application's business rather than the host's. As with Bun, being listed here means a provider says so itself, not that the runtime happens to work.
