lead: Their racks, their responsibility.
---

Owning the machines means the provider chooses the hardware, the data center and the network, and carries the cost of all three. It usually shows up as better price-performance and as a real answer to where data physically sits. It also means capacity is finite in a way a hyperscaler's is not, which is a trade rather than a fault.
