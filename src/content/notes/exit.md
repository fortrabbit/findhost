This records the longest a provider can keep charging after a customer decides to stop: the minimum term, the notice period, or the remainder of a prepaid year. It is the other half of what a price costs, and the half that is never on the pricing page — a cheap plan that cannot be left for twelve months is not cheap.

Monthly billing with cancellation at any time lands in the first band. Annual contracts, prepaid discounts and enterprise agreements land in the last ones. The band describes the standard published terms, not what a sales conversation might achieve, and it is a fact about the contract rather than a criticism of it: a long term is often what pays for a lower [entry price](/entry-price/).
