description: How long a hosting bill keeps arriving after the decision to leave — notice periods and minimum terms, from a day to over a year.
---

Recorded here is the longest a provider can keep charging after a customer decides to stop: the minimum term, the notice period, or the remainder of a prepaid year. It is the other half of what a price costs and the half that never appears on the pricing page, because a cheap plan that cannot be left for twelve months is not cheap. Monthly billing with cancellation at any time lands in the first band; annual contracts and enterprise agreements land in the last. A long term is a fact about the contract rather than a criticism of it — it is often what pays for a lower [entry price](/entry-price/).
