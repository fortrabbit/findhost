---
description: Whether a provider offers a permanent free tier, a trial only, or nothing free at all.
lead: Free forever, free for a fortnight, or not free.
---

Three answers. **Permanent** means something keeps running without payment, however small. **Trial** means free access that ends on a date or at a quota. **None** means the meter starts at signup.

A free tier is a marketing instrument as much as a product, and it is recorded rather than rewarded: it says nothing about what the paid plans cost, how the platform behaves under load, or whether the free thing resembles what would eventually be paid for. It is also not part of the [entry price](/entry-price/) scale, which measures what a provider charges rather than what it gives away. Where a trial is the deciding factor, it is usually because it settles a question no comparison table can: whether the thing actually runs.
