The free account is the product here, not a [free tier](/pricing/free-tier/) under a paid plan or a [free trial](/pricing/free-trial/) with a clock on it. Something else pays: ads on the control panel, commission when a site outgrows the account, or supporters. The caps that matter are rarely the advertised ones — requests a day, files on disk and processes at once decide whether a busy site stays up, and [a shell](/shell/) is rare. Some of these brands are one wholesaler's panel and servers under another name.
