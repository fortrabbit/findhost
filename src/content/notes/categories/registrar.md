description: Companies that register domain names, whether or not they also host the site the name points at.
lead: The other invoice, and usually the first one.
---

A site needs a name before it needs a server, and the two are often bought from different companies. This marks the ones that sell the name. Many also host — the domain brings the customer and the hosting is the second thing in the basket — so a record here commonly carries a hosting category too. Whether a domain comes free *with* a plan is a separate question, recorded as [a feature](/features/domains/) rather than as this.
