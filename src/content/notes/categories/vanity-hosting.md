Some software makes particular demands of its hosting — integrated caching, image delivery, updates applied for it — and this category bundles them and tunes them for the one application it runs. The term is coined here; the usual vocabulary has no word for the shape. WordPress attracts the most of it, with Craft CMS, TYPO3 and Shopware at a smaller scale, and sometimes the makers host their own software, which is how [Laravel Cloud](/laravel-cloud/) and [Craft Cloud](/craft-cloud/) came about. The trade is that a stack tuned for one application cannot run another, so leaving is a migration rather than a redeploy.
