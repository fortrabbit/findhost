Joomla support in practice means the control panel offers it: an installer entry beside WordPress and Drupal on a shared hosting plan. That is how most Joomla sites were built and how most are still updated. The technical requirements are ordinary PHP and MySQL, so a provider listing it is signaling its audience rather than any particular capability.
