Shopware is the shop platform of the German-speaking market and asks for much the same as Magento: a build step, a search service, a cache, and background workers for indexing and mail. Hosting is concentrated with providers who know that stack, mostly in the same market. A generic PHP plan will install it and struggle under any real catalog.
