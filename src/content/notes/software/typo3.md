TYPO3 has a concentrated audience: agencies and public bodies, largely in German-speaking Europe, running sites that live for a decade. Hosting for it follows the same shape — a Composer-based deployment, long-term support versions, and providers who know the release cycle. Outside that market almost nobody documents it, which is why the count here is small and says more about geography than about the software.
