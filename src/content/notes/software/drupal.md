lead: A CMS deployed like an application.
---

Drupal is managed with Composer and deployed like an application rather than uploaded like a site, so what matters is whether a host supports a build step and a writable files directory that survives it. Major-version upgrades are projects rather than button presses, which is why Drupal hosting clusters at the professional end: the providers documenting it tend to sell to agencies and public sector buyers who plan those upgrades in advance.
