---

Kirby stores content as files, which removes the database from the hosting question entirely and makes it one of the easier things to run: PHP, a writable content directory, and a backup that copies files rather than dumps tables. Almost any [PHP](/runtimes/php/) host can serve it. The short list here reflects who has written it down, not who can run it.
