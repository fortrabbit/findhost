WooCommerce is WordPress, so every provider here also appears under [WordPress](/software/wordpress/), but the shop changes the shape of the load. Cart and checkout pages cannot be served from cache, which turns a mostly-static site into one where every visitor hits PHP and the database. Providers documenting WooCommerce specifically usually mean object caching, a database sized for it, and enough headroom for a sale that goes well.
