Magento, now Adobe Commerce, is demanding in a way most PHP applications are not: a long build, high memory limits, Elasticsearch or OpenSearch for the catalog, Redis for sessions and cache, and background processes for indexing. Small plans do not run it, whatever the language support says. Providers documenting it are specialists, and their pricing reflects the infrastructure rather than the software.
