Who the provider is selling to. Audience is about the commercial relationship. This is how support is staffed, whether there is a contract to negotiate, if an invoice can satisfy a procurement department, how much hand-holding the documentation assumes. Two providers can run the same software for the same kind of site and be completely unusable for each other's customers. This is why it is a separate field from [use case](/use-case/). A CMS is a CMS whether it belongs to a freelancer or a ministry; the purchasing, the compliance paperwork and the escalation path are not. The size and shape of the buyer, rather than the job title of the person at the keyboard: a freelancer is the smallest kind of business, and whether a platform expects a developer is a question [category](/categories/).
