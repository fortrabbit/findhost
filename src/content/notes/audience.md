Audience is about the commercial relationship rather than the technology: how support is staffed, whether there is a contract to negotiate, whether an invoice can satisfy a procurement department, how much hand-holding the documentation assumes. Two providers can run the same software for the same kind of site and be completely unusable for each other's customers.

This is why it is a separate field from [use case](/use-case/). A CMS is a CMS whether it belongs to a freelancer or a ministry; the purchasing, the compliance paperwork and the escalation path are not. One axis, the size and shape of the buyer, rather than the job title of the person at the keyboard: a freelancer is the smallest kind of business, and whether a platform expects a developer is a question [category](/categories/) already answers. As with use case, the value is what the provider says about itself.
