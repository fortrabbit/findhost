Infrastructure as a Service, is a cloud computing model where customers rent computing resources such as servers, storage, and networking equipment. Customers create and manage their own virtual data centers in the cloud, without having to purchase and maintain physical hardware. This allows for scalability, flexibility, and cost savings for large businesses and organizations.

In cloud computing acronyms, IaaS is the foundational layer. On top of that is [PaaS](/category/paas/), followed by the SaaS layer.
